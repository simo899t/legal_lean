As a presentable MVP, we will start out focusing on the Danish traffic law, as an area of law that can be easier to implement mechanically / is often determinable relatively objectively on the facts, without need for too much interpretation or human jugement.

SWOT Analysis

Opportunities:
Structuring legal paragraphs
- for AI training
- as source of reference

Threats:
Cases: Pseudonymized != anonymized
(Pseudonymized not equal to anonymized, so will EU e.g. accept training AI on actual case materials?)
